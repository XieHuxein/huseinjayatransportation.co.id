About Husein Jaya Transport

Providing comfortable, reliable, and modern intercity bus transportation across Indonesia with premium services and seamless online ticket booking.

Husein Jaya Transport is committed to delivering a safe, comfortable, and enjoyable travel experience. Our modern fleet is designed to meet the needs of every passenger, from daily business trips to long-distance vacations.

  • Modern Executive, Sleeper & Premium Coaches.
  • Professional Drivers & Experienced Crew.
  • Comfortable Seats with Premium Facilities.
  • Easy Online Ticket Booking Anytime.

Since our establishment, we have focused on providing high-quality land transportation by combining reliable operations with modern technology. Through our online booking platform, passengers can easily browse schedules, select seats, and manage reservations in just a few simple steps.

AVANTE D1

Built by Tentrem and mounted on the legendary Scania K410IB chassis, this low-drive coach was previously operated by Easy Trans before joining the HJT fleet. Its distinctive livery, retained from its previous operator, makes this unit instantly recognizable among the rest of the fleet.

JETBUS 5 SDD

Fleet number DD-001 marks HJT's very first double-decker coach. Built on the powerful Mercedes-Benz O500RSD 2445 Euro 4 chassis, it represents the beginning of HJT's premium double-decker journey.

Fazrevolver SHD

Fleet number HJP-003 is the third coach in HJT's tourism division. Built on the Hino RM 280 ABS chassis, it is designed to provide reliable performance and comfort for charter and tourism services.

JETBUS 2 HD

One of HJT's premium executive coaches, featuring the iconic High Deck Single Glass body with a length of 12.8 meters. Powered by the reliable Scania K360IB chassis, this coach delivers outstanding comfort, stability, and performance for long-distance journeys.

JETBUS 5 SDD

Carrying fleet number DD-013, this coach is HJT's first Scania-powered double decker. Built on the Scania K450CB Euro 4 chassis, it combines premium performance with the latest double-decker technology.

JETBUS 3 SHD

Fleet number HJP-002 is the second coach in HJT's tourism fleet. Purchased brand-new directly from the coachbuilder, this unit features a unique livery that distinguishes it from HJP-003. It is powered by a custom air-suspension Hino RK8 R260 chassis.

JETBUS 3+ SHD

This stylish Super High Deck coach features a unique side-panel modification without the traditional side skirt, providing passengers with a wider outside view. Combined with its Single Glass design and the iconic Mercedes-Benz O500RS 1836 chassis with a signature center door, it offers both elegance and comfort.

JETBUS 2+ SDD

A special anniversary edition created to celebrate HJT's milestone, easily recognized by its exclusive commemorative livery. This unique coach is built on the dependable Scania K410IB chassis.

All New Avante H8

Fleet number HJP-001 is one of HJT's most iconic coaches. Known as the "Omah Mlaku" (Moving House), its interior has been extensively customized into a home-like living space, offering a truly unique travel experience. The coach is built on the Mercedes-Benz OH1626L Euro 4 chassis.
